About the music

These songs are made with Voices AI and reviewed by Roger Keyserling before they are published. The themes run through the whole Federation: code with a heart, machines that remember, and people who refuse to be broken. Rock, back-road grit and electronic glitch, all with one question underneath. What is more than code?
